Image Composition Grading

PythonPyTorchComputer Vision

Fine-tuned ResNet-18 with a regression head to predict the aesthetic composition score of a photograph on a 1–5 scale, trained on the CADB dataset of 10,000+ images. Used transfer learning with frozen early layers, data augmentation, early stopping, and hyperparameter tuning. Final model achieved 0.36 MAE and 0.21 MSE on the test split. Built as a UTMIST Machine Learning Fundamentals project.
